About data views and outlier detection

All view combines Firebase prices with local synced and pending/unverified scrapes. A stored-price match is shown separately from a confirmed sync. Local audit shows raw source text from this browser. Older Firebase history contains parsed prices only. Dates use Qatar time.

Outliers differ by more than 10% from a nearby same-ticker cluster: at least 3 supporting prices and a two-thirds majority within 5 minutes. Missing nearby consensus does not flag a price. Early legacy records also get forward, backward, combined and repeatable sampled checks against later prices with recorded column provenance; historical flags require stronger agreement and an extreme discrepancy. Removal applies to the current filters, up to 100 records at a time.
